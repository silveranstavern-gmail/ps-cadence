P.S. | Cadence

This complete offline installer includes all six voices, the speech model, and the software needed to run Cadence. No Python installation, account, or internet connection is required.

Cadence uses compatible NVIDIA graphics automatically and can also generate speech on your CPU. All included voices work without an internet connection.

Your readings, settings, and generated audio are saved separately from the application. Updating or uninstalling Cadence keeps that library.
